# Business formation checklist — Colorado (2026-11-23)

Not legal or tax advice. Third-party fee figures flagged; confirm on the official site at filing time.

## 1. Entity type
| Type | Protects personal assets? | Cost to form | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | No | $0 (+ trade name $20) | Too risky: vehicles, remains, grieving clients |
| **LLC (Limited Liability Company)** | Yes, if kept separate | ~$50 + $25/yr report | **Pick.** Simple, flexible taxes |
| LLC taxed as S-corp | Yes | same + payroll | Later, once profit ~ $40k+/yr (ask a CPA) |
| C-corp | Yes | $50 + more paperwork | Only if raising venture money |
Name confirmed available: "The Golden Gates Company LLC" (coloradosos.gov, 2026-11-23).

## 2. Colorado SOS Articles of Organization (online only)
- Fee ~$50; periodic report ~$25/yr (third-party figures).
- Jason must supply: principal office street address, mailing address, registered agent (name + Colorado street
  address, no P.O. box), name of person forming. All become public record.
- Registered agent: Jason can serve himself (ID required since 2025 to prove CO residency; home address becomes public),
  or pay a commercial agent (~$100–$150/yr) to keep his home address private. Decision needed.
- Claude fills the form; Jason reviews; Claude stops at payment.

## 3. EIN (Employer Identification Number = the business's federal tax ID)
- ONLY at irs.gov/EIN. Free, issued instantly online, weekdays ~7am–10pm ET.
- FTC warns lookalike sites charge up to $300 for the same free number.
- Jason types his SSN himself. Download the confirmation letter (CP 575) as PDF.
- Do this AFTER the LLC is approved.

## 4. Colorado Department of Revenue (CDOR)
- State sales tax license via MyBizColorado if selling goods (urns, keepsakes, paw prints).
  Fee $12 (Jul–Dec 2026) or $16, plus $50 refundable deposit. Transport/concierge services alone are likely non-taxable; goods are.
- Since 1/1/2026 retailers can't keep the state vendor fee.

## 5. City licenses
- Lakewood: all home-based businesses, including service businesses, need a Sales or Use Tax License. $20, 2 years,
  apply 30+ days before starting ($30 late charge). Portal: Lakewood Business Pro. Home occupation zoning allowed.
  City pages conflict on service-only; confirm with Lakewood Finance 303-987-7630.
- Denver: delivering taxable goods into Denver requires a Denver retailer's use tax license (no fee, 2-year).
  Service-only out-of-city status unclear; confirm with Denver Taxpayer Service 720-913-9400.

## 6. Operating agreement
- Not filed with the state but banks ask for it. Single-member LLC version. Draft pending (size: ~6 pages, PDF).

## 7. Business bank account checklist
- Articles of Organization (stamped copy from SOS)
- EIN confirmation letter (CP 575)
- Operating agreement (signed)
- Jason's driver's license (he brings it; never stored here)
- Opening deposit (often $0–$100)
- Ask: monthly fee and how to waive it, mobile deposit on Android, Stripe/Shopify payout compatibility, debit card for fuel.
