# Colorado pet-transport and pet-cremation rules — first pass (2026-11-23)

Not legal advice. Each item lists what was found and what still needs a phone call.

## 1. Pet Animal Care and Facilities Act (PACFA), Colorado Dept. of Agriculture (CDA)
- PACFA licenses facilities that keep animals for adoption, breeding, boarding, grooming, selling, sheltering, trading or transferring (C.R.S. Title 35, Art. 80; rules 8 CCR 1202-15).
- "Pet transporter" = anyone who accepts pet animals for transport "for the purpose of adoption, rescue, selling, harboring, sheltering, trading, or otherwise transferring." That reads as LIVE animals, so deceased-pet transport most likely falls outside it.
- OPEN: confirm in writing with the CDA PACFA program that deceased-pet transport needs no PACFA license. If Golden Gates ever moves LIVE pets (e.g. to a home-euthanasia vet), a PACFA license applies.
- PACFA had a sunset review in 2025 (DORA/COPRRR); check current status.

## 2. Pet cremation
- No Colorado statute specifically regulating pet crematories was found. HB25-1217 (funeral consumer protections) covers HUMAN funeral services only.
- Model to follow anyway: Maryland's 2026 Pet Cremation and Burial Services Consumer Protection Act requires a written description of private vs. partitioned vs. communal cremation. Golden Gates should do this voluntarily: it builds trust and protects against "whose ashes?" disputes.
- If Golden Gates OPERATES a cremation unit: CDPHE (Colorado Dept. of Public Health and Environment) requires an Air Pollutant Emission Notice (APEN), Crematory/Incinerator form APCD-234, and possibly an air permit; plus local zoning.
- Recommended launch model: partner with an existing licensed crematory; Golden Gates handles transport and concierge only. No APEN needed.

## 3. Transport vehicle
- No Colorado rule found that governs carrying a deceased pet in a vehicle. Live-animal rules (secured in vehicle) don't address remains.
- Practical must-haves: commercial auto insurance, a sealed and cooled transport, a chain-of-custody form (tag at pickup, signed at handoff).

## 4. Local
- Denver: city collects dead animals through Solid Waste Management (720-913-1311), so no Denver ordinance bans private aftercare transport was found. Check the Denver Revised Municipal Code and business license needs.
