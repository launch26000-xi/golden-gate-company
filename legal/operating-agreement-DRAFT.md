# OPERATING AGREEMENT OF THE GOLDEN GATES COMPANY LLC
### A Colorado Single-Member Limited Liability Company — DRAFT for review (not legal advice)

**Effective date:** [date Articles of Organization are filed]

## 1. Formation
1.1 **Name.** The Golden Gates Company LLC (the "Company").
1.2 **Formation.** Formed under the Colorado Limited Liability Company Act (C.R.S. § 7-80-101 et seq.) by filing Articles of Organization with the Colorado Secretary of State.
1.3 **Principal office.** [business street address], or another place the Member chooses.
1.4 **Registered agent.** As listed with the Colorado Secretary of State, as updated from time to time.
1.5 **Term.** Perpetual, until dissolved under Section 9.

## 2. Purpose
The Company provides pet aftercare transport, memorial concierge and related services and goods, and may do any lawful business.

## 3. Member
3.1 **Sole Member.** Jason Thompson (the "Member") owns 100% of the Company.
3.2 **Additional members.** Only by written amendment signed by the Member.

## 4. Capital
4.1 **Initial contribution.** $[amount] in cash and/or property listed in Schedule A.
4.2 **No required further contributions.** The Member may contribute more but is not required to.
4.3 **No interest** is paid on contributions.

## 5. Management
5.1 **Member-managed.** The Member has full authority to run the Company, sign contracts, open accounts, and hire.
5.2 **Officers.** The Member may appoint officers with titles and duties set in writing.
5.3 **Records.** The Company keeps its books, this Agreement, filings and tax returns at its principal office.

## 6. Separateness (protects limited liability)
The Company will: keep its own bank account; never mix personal and Company funds; sign contracts in the Company's name ("The Golden Gates Company LLC, by Jason Thompson, Member"); keep its own records; carry appropriate insurance (including commercial auto); and file its Colorado periodic report on time.

## 7. Allocations, distributions and taxes
7.1 **Profits and losses** belong 100% to the Member.
7.2 **Distributions** at the Member's discretion, only if the Company can still pay its debts.
7.3 **Tax status.** Treated as a disregarded entity for federal income tax unless the Member elects otherwise (for example, S-corporation status) after consulting a tax professional.

## 8. Liability and indemnification
8.1 **Limited liability.** The Member is not personally liable for Company debts solely because of being a Member.
8.2 **Indemnification.** The Company indemnifies the Member and officers for acts in good faith on the Company's behalf, except gross negligence, willful misconduct or knowing violation of law.

## 9. Dissolution
9.1 **Events.** Written decision of the Member, or as required by law.
9.2 **Winding up.** Pay creditors first, then distribute the rest to the Member; file Articles of Dissolution.
